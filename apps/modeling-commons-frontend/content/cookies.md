# Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how the Modeling Commons uses cookies. It should be read alongside our [Privacy Policy](/privacy) and [Terms of Service](/terms), which use the same definitions.

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They are used to remember your preferences, maintain your session, and help us understand how the Service is used. Cookies may be set by the Service itself (first-party cookies) or by third-party services operating on our behalf (third-party cookies).

### Cookies We Use

The table below lists the cookies set by the Modeling Commons and the third-party services we use.

#### Required Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Service to function. They cannot be disabled without breaking core functionality.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_Secure-better-auth.session_token` | Modeling Commons | Authenticates your session and keeps you logged in. | Session |
| `__Secure-better-auth.dont_remember` | Modeling Commons | Records that you chose not to persist your login across browser sessions. | Session |
| `cf_clearance` | Cloudflare | Verifies that you have passed Cloudflare's bot protection challenge. Required to access the Service. | Up to 1 year |

#### Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors use the Service so we can improve it.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics | Distinguishes unique visitors to the Service. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). | 2 years |
| `_mc_uid` | Modeling Commons | An anonymized tracking identifier used for internal usage analytics. Generated using secure hash functions with daily-rotated salts. This cookie cannot be used to identify you personally. | Rotated daily |

#### Legacy Cookies

The following cookie may still be present from a previous version of the Service. It is no longer actively set.

| Cookie | Provider | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `__utma` | Google Analytics (Universal Analytics) | Previously used for visitor tracking under the legacy analytics system. No longer in use. |

If this cookie is present in your browser, you may safely delete it. It will not be re-set.

### Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may set cookies:

**Google Analytics.** We use Google Analytics to collect aggregated usage statistics. Google may transfer and store this data on its servers. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing [Google's opt-out browser add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout). For details, see [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Cloudflare.** We use Cloudflare for security and performance. Cloudflare sets the `cf_clearance` cookie to verify legitimate traffic. For details, see [Cloudflare's privacy policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

### Controlling Cookies

Most browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

- View what cookies are stored and delete them individually.
- Block third-party cookies.
- Block all cookies from specific sites.
- Block all cookies entirely.
- Delete all cookies when you close your browser.

If you block required cookies, you will not be able to log in to the Service. If you block analytics cookies, the Service will still function normally.

For more information on managing cookies, visit [aboutcookies.org](https://www.aboutcookies.org).
